# Partner Tier Outline (v0.1)

**Aligns to:** `docs/strategy/03_revenue_model.md` · **Owners:** G2 + A2

| Tier | Who | Ops implication |
|------|-----|-----------------|
| T0 | TruTravel platform | Demand, trust, payouts, brand |
| T1 | Anchor operators | Full delivery; QA gate; settlement 55–65% of P |
| T2 | Experience affiliates | Add-ons (stays, gear, passes); rev-share on attach |
| T3 | Community captains | Culture + seat fill; credits then bounty |
| T4 | Referral affiliates | Demand gen; pay on **completed** trips only |
| T5 | Traveler members | Loyalty credits funded from platform take |
