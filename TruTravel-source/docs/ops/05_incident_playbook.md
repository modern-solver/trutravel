# Incident Playbook (v0.1)

**Owner:** A4 + G2 · **Guardrail:** Safety incidents unresolved > 48h = **0** (scope metric)

## Severity

| Level | Examples | Response SLA |
|-------|----------|--------------|
| S1 Critical | Life safety, missing person, sexual assault report, major medical | Immediate; founder + ops bridge |
| S2 High | Injury, theft, stranding, severe harassment | < 2h acknowledge |
| S3 Medium | Supplier no-show, housing failure, ticket fraud | < 8h |
| S4 Low | Complaints, mild conflict | < 24h |

## Channels

1. In-app emergency (MVP: published phone + WhatsApp bridge OK if logged)  
2. Captain → Ops hotline  
3. Operator escalation  

## S1 immediate actions

1. Ensure traveler safety / local emergency services  
2. Open incident ticket (time, corridor, segment, departure ID)  
3. Notify founder + on-call  
4. Do not public-comment on social without counsel  
5. Preserve evidence (chats, bookings)  
6. Family/emergency contact only with traveler consent or legal necessity  

## Segment-specific notes

- **Thrilling:** evacuation / insurance activation  
- **Trippy:** substance policy enforcement; no illegal facilitation  
- **Couple:** privacy of reporting party  
- **CodeHouses:** house eviction protocol, lock change, cowork device theft  
- **Festival:** crowd separation plan, lost traveler meetup pin (time-limited)  

## Post-incident

- [ ] Traveler care follow-up  
- [ ] Refund/comp decision per matrix  
- [ ] Partner penalty or delist review  
- [ ] Blameless write-up → update QA checklist
