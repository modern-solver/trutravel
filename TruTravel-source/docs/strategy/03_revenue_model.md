# TruTravel — Hierarchical Revenue Model

## Design goals

1. Incentivize **local partners**, **affiliate businesses**, and **volunteers**.  
2. Keep **platform contribution margin** healthy (target **≥ 18–25%** of GMV after all variable incentives).  
3. Prefer recurring / network effects over one-off ads.  
4. Align incentives so better hosts earn more, not louder marketers.

## Value stack (who captures what)

```
Traveler pays trip price (P)
        │
        ├─ Local Partner (ops cost + partner margin) ........ ~55–68% of P
        ├─ Affiliate referral cut (if attributed) .......... 2–5% of P
        ├─ Volunteer / community host rewards .............. 1–3% of P (or credits)
        ├─ Platform take (TruTravel) ....................... 12–22% of P
        └─ Payment + trust/insurance rail .................. 2–4% of P
```

**Floor rule:** Platform net after payment fees never drops below **10% of P** on standard trips; premium/curated can take higher.

## Partner hierarchy (tiers)

| Tier | Who | Role | Economics |
|------|-----|------|-----------|
| **T0 Platform** | TruTravel | Matching, trust, payments, brand, demand | Take rate 12–22% by segment & exclusivity |
| **T1 Anchor Operators** | Licensed local tour ops | Full trip delivery, insurance-ready | 55–65% of P; volume bonuses; preferred placement |
| **T2 Experience Affiliates** | Stays, gear, cafes, studios, transport, festival passes | Upsells & add-ons | 8–15% commission on affiliate GMV to platform; partner keeps rest |
| **T3 Community Captains (volunteers → pro)** | Verified trip leaders / vibe hosts | Fill seats, culture, safety liaison | Credits + 1–3% seat bounty; path to paid Host Pro |
| **T4 Referral Affiliates** | Creators, campus ambs, micro-influencers | Demand gen | 3–8% of first booking or flat bounty |
| **T5 Traveler Members** | End users | Book, review, rebook | Loyalty: 1–2% credit pool funded from platform take |

## Segment-tuned take rates (illustrative)

| Segment | Typical AOV (India, 3–7d) | Platform take | Rationale |
|---------|---------------------------|---------------|-----------|
| Trippy Tours | ₹12–25k | 14–18% | Higher service load, vibe hosts |
| Thrilling Tours | ₹15–40k | 12–16% | Higher COGS (permits, gear); protect partner |
| Wellness Tours | ₹20–50k | 16–22% | Higher willingness-to-pay, brand premium |
| Couple Getaways | ₹25–60k (for 2) | 15–20% | Privacy + curation premium |
| CodeHouses | ₹18–45k (week+) | 15–20% | Longer stays, cowork stack, higher attach (wifi/desk/meals) |
| Music + Art Festivals | ₹10–35k (+ ticket) | 12–18% | Ticket/pass affiliate attach; peak season density; logistics load |

## Incentive waterfall (example ₹20,000 seat)

| Line item | % | ₹ |
|-----------|---|---|
| Traveler price | 100% | 20,000 |
| Local partner settlement | 62% | 12,400 |
| Payment gateway + escrow buffer | 2.5% | 500 |
| Community captain bounty | 1.5% | 300 |
| Demand affiliate (if any) | 3% | 600 |
| **TruTravel gross** | **31%** | **6,200** |
| Less: support, refunds reserve, marketing CAC allocation | ~10–12 pts | ~2,000–2,400 |
| **Contribution toward fixed opex** | **~19–21%** | **~3,800–4,200** |

*If no affiliate: platform keeps the 3% → improves margin.*  
*Volume bonus for T1: +1–2 pts from platform marketing budget, not from partner settlement.*

## Five distinct revenue sources

1. **Primary take rate on group-trip GMV** (core marketplace commission).  
2. **Affiliate / add-on attach** (stays upgrades, gear, experiences, transfers, festival passes).  
3. **Demand-side referral & creator program** (performance marketing with controlled bounties).  
4. **Operator tools subscription (Host Pro)** — seat management, boosts, analytics (after supply density).  
5. **Trust & attach products** — insurance rev-share, priority support tiers, verified-badge programs for partners.

## Non-trip revenue (layered, not day-1 critical)

1. Featured placement / boost for operators (capped so trust isn’t sold out).  
2. Host Pro SaaS for serious locals.  
3. Brand / destination partnerships (state tourism, outdoor brands) — sponsored corridors, not fake reviews.  
4. Insurance & add-on attach.  
5. B2B offsites under Wellness / Thrilling / CodeHouses (later).

## Margin guards (hard rules)

- Never discount platform take below floor without founder approval.  
- Volunteer bounties paid in **credits first** until trip completes + review window.  
- Refunds: partner and platform share per policy matrix (no full platform eat-all).  
- Affiliates paid on **net completed trips**, not clicks.  
- Quarterly review of take rates per segment using contribution margin dashboards.
