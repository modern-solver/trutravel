# TruTravel — SWOT, Revenues & Short-Term Milestone Plan

## SWOT analysis

| | Helpful | Harmful |
|--|---------|---------|
| **Internal** | **Strengths:** Clear 6-segment moat (incl. CodeHouses + Festivals); dual community+commerce thesis; hierarchical incentives align locals/volunteers; technocratic UI differentiation; India density path | **Weaknesses:** Cold-start (supply & demand); trust/safety burden; ops-heavy across more archetypes; brand unknown vs OTAs; capital needs for refunds/marketing |
| **External** | **Opportunities:** Post-pandemic group & experience demand; creator economy affiliates; festival calendars; builder/workation wave; state tourism partnerships; underserved “find my people” gap; wellness & couple premium pricing | **Threats:** Copy by Thrillophilia/Tripoto; WhatsApp group informality; regulatory/tourism licensing; festival ticket fraud; seasonality; safety incidents; race-to-bottom competitors |

## Five distinct revenue sources

1. Primary take rate on group-trip GMV  
2. Affiliate / add-on attach  
3. Demand-side referral & creator program  
4. Operator tools subscription (Host Pro)  
5. Trust & attach products (insurance, verified badges, priority support)  

## Short-term milestone plan (mini-Groks)

Horizon: **12 weeks** to public MVP beta in **1–2 India corridors × all 6 segments (thin inventory)**.

### M0 — Foundation (Week 0–1)

**Owners:** Orchestrator, A1, A3, P1, G3  

**Outputs:** Formalize docs; segment definitions + matching tags; corridor shortlist (Himachal/Uttarakhand + Goa/Gokarna or Kerala); brand one-pager + UI principles  

**Exit:** Founder sign-off on corridors + segment cards  

### M1 — Strategy freeze & economics (Week 1–2)

**Owners:** A2, A4, G2, P1  

**Outputs:** Seat waterfall v1 + refund matrix; partner tier contracts outline; risk register MVP must-haves; PRD for vertical slice #1  

**Exit:** Unit economics model greenlit; legal checklist owned  

### M2 — Design system & clickable prototype (Week 2–4)

**Owners:** P2, P3, P1, A3  

**Outputs:** Design tokens + segment visual OS; key screens; matching v0 rubric  

**Exit:** Usability walkthrough with 5 target users  

### M3 — Platform vertical slice (Week 3–7)

**Owners:** T1, T2, T3, T4, T5  

**Outputs:** Auth, profiles, catalog, booking+pay test mode, ledger stubs; event instrumentation; admin trip/partner tools  

**Exit:** One paid test booking end-to-end in staging  

### M4 — Supply pack & captains (Week 4–8)

**Owners:** G2, G1, A4  

**Outputs:** ≥ 8–12 QA’d trips across 6 segments; ≥ 10 captains; operator onboarding checklist  

**Exit:** Supply calendar for first 30 days of beta  

### M5 — Closed beta (Week 8–10)

**Owners:** G1, G3, T5, A2  

**Outputs:** 200–500 invite users; 30–50 seats booked; NPS + margin dashboard  

**Exit:** Decision: expand corridors vs deepen conversion  

### M6 — Public MVP & learning loop (Week 10–12)

**Owners:** All  

**Outputs:** Public web launch (limited corridors); growth playbooks per segment; Host Pro wishlist  

**Exit:** KPI review vs scope metrics; Q2 roadmap
